About Kalolsavam

The 64th edition of Asia's largest cultural festival for students is currently being held inThrissur from January 14 to 18, 2026. With 25 venues named after Kerala's major rivers, the festival showcases the incredible talents of over 14,000 students across 239 events.The Kerala School Kalolsavam is unique in its four-tier structure, where students progress from School level to Sub-District, District, and finally the State level. This prestigious event, started in 1956, continues to be the heartbeat of Kerala's youth culture.
